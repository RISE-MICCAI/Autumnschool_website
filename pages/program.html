Learning to Self-Organize: Neural Cellular Automata for Medical Imaging

presented by John Kalkhof John Kalkhof
John Kalkhof is a postdoctoral researcher at Inria, working on lightweight and robust AI methods for medical imaging and disease progression modeling with a focus on accessibility and equity. His work has been recognized with the IPMI Francois Erbsmann Prize and the MICCAI Young Scientist Award. , Henry J. Krumb Henry J. Krumb
Henry Krumb is a first year postdoctoral researcher at Technische Universität Darmstadt, where he investigates AI methods in medical image processing and computer-assisted surgery. A particular focus of his work is on integrating Neural Cellular Automata (NCA) into surgical pipelines. , Marawan Elbatel Marawan Elbatel
Marawan Elbatel is a PhD candidate at HKUST. His research focuses on data-efficient medical image analysis, enabling reliable diagnosis from accessible imaging, particularly in African healthcare settings. and Jonas Mehtali Jonas Mehtali
Jonas Mehtali is a PhD student at the University of Strasbourg supervised by Caroline Essert. His research focuses on computer-assisted thermal ablation planning, and he developed C-NCA, an NCA-based method for fast simulation, presented at MICCAI 2025.
